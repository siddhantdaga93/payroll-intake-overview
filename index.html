The checks your team does by hand, done on every payroll

The AI only reads the form. Every check is plain arithmetic, the same each time, and explained in words when it fails.

Hours add upDaily hours match the weekly total, straight time and overtime, with no impossible days.
Pay adds upHours × rate plus fringe equals gross; gross minus deductions equals net pay.
Dates fit the weekEvery day worked falls inside the week being reported.
Duplicates caughtThe same week filed twice, or the same worker listed twice, is called out.
Nothing missingContractor, project and week ending present; every worker has a name and hours.
Private by designOnly the last four digits of a Social Security number are ever kept.
